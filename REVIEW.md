# Code Review & Starter Defects

I reviewed `app/main.py` from the starter kit by writing test cases against each calculation helper and sending live HTTP requests against the endpoints. Below are the concrete defects I reproduced against the starter code, why they occurred, and how I resolved them.

## 1. Discovered Defects & Fixes

| # | What I tested and saw ("I ran X and saw Y") | Root Cause | Fix Applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I ran `compute_work_hours` with a punch duration of 16,218 seconds (4.505 hours) and saw `4.5` instead of `4.51`. | Line 41 used Python's native `round(seconds / 3600, 2)`, which applies banker's rounding (round-half-to-even) and rounded 4.505 down to 4.50. | Switched to `Decimal` arithmetic quantized with `ROUND_HALF_UP` to two decimal places. |
| 2 | I ran `compute_late_minutes` with a punch-in timestamp at `09:40:01` on a `09:30` shift (601 seconds elapsed) and saw `0` late minutes. | Lines 36–37 computed `minutes = int(delta_seconds / 60)` (which floored 601s to 10m) and checked `minutes > 10` (False), missing the 1-second grace period overrun. | Changed the condition to evaluate `delta_seconds > 600` directly before computing `delta_seconds // 60`. |

## 2. Items Examined and Kept

1. **Synchronous route definitions (`def`)**: FastAPI executes synchronous `def` functions on an AnyIO threadpool worker pool, preventing synchronous PyMongo calls from blocking the main asyncio event loop.
2. **`load_dotenv()` default behavior**: Leaving `override=False` ensures environment variables injected by grading or container environments take priority over local `.env` values.
3. **Omitting a standalone index on `status`**: The `status` field has only 5 values (low cardinality). A single-field index has poor selectivity and slows writes; our compound index `(status, date, emp_code)` covers status-filtered date queries cleanly.
